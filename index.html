🔬 How to get research experience as a high-schooler

Step by step

  1. Pick a question you genuinely care about. Start small and specific.
  2. Read 5–10 articles (Google Scholar, university news) to learn what's known.
  3. Find a mentor: a science teacher, a parent's colleague, or a university researcher (email template →).
  4. Follow the rules: research on people, animals or hazardous materials needs approval under science-fair (ISEF/TXSEF) rules.
  5. Present at your school or regional fair → TXSEF → Regeneron ISEF.
  6. Publish in a reputable student journal (free or low-fee, peer-reviewed).
